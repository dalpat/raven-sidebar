Raven Sidebar · UI Revamp

Three directions — full sidebars, side by side

Each direction is rendered complete and to scale on a desktop wallpaper, so you can compare entire designs at once — nothing hidden below a fold. Toggle light/dark and the Widgets/Notifications view from the bar below.

Theme
View
1
AuroraRefined dark
Safest · fastest

A faithful evolution of today’s sidebar — same layout, unified into one design system: consistent 14px cards, real slider handles, quiet section labels, and the indigo→violet accent used throughout.

  • Lowest build risk — pure CSS refresh, no new widgets or wiring.
  • Section grouping gives the stack hierarchy it lacks today.
  • Light mode is a designed state, not a tint.
Pick this if you want a clear, low-risk improvement you can ship this week.
2
GlassControl center
Most modern

A translucent, blurred panel floating over the wallpaper, with a quick-toggle grid (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, DND, Night Light) up top — turning the sidebar into a proper GNOME-style control center.

  • Quick toggles add real functionality, not just styling.
  • Frosted blur matches GNOME’s adaptive aesthetic; highest visual impact.
  • More effort: St blur needs a shell backdrop effect, and toggles mean new NM / D-Bus wiring.
Pick this if you want the most premium feel and accept extra build work.
3
CompactInformation dense
Power users

Narrower and tighter: a smaller clock, condensed rows, a 2-column toggle grid and slim sliders — everything visible at a glance with minimal scrolling. Monochrome with a single accent stripe.

  • Most data per pixel, least scrolling, smaller screen footprint.
  • Toggle grid + slim controls keep it dense but legible.
  • Trade-off: less breathing room; the clock is no longer a hero.
Pick this if you live in the sidebar and value density over flourish.
